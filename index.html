Drefvelin Regnskap

Drefvelin Regnskap is a private, internal bookkeeping tool for Drefvelin Development (enkeltpersonforetak, Norway). It is not offered to the public.

The tool reads receipts and invoices from the company's own Gmail accounts, with read-only access, and stores them as accounting documentation as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven).
